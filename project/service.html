Our Services

Agricultural Produce Purchase

In our company, we engage in the purchase of high quality farm produce from farmers. We go to the extra mile to ensure we purchase quality products from farmers at a highly competitive price that will ensure we get the best raw materials for our customers while also making sure that the farmers are also making good profit from the sales that made to us. Our utmost priority is obtaining the best quality of cocoa products and the happiness of the farmers.

Sale of Farm Produce

At lekanMath Nigeria Enterprise, we also engage in the sales of high quality farm produce including cocoa beans which is used as raw materials in various industries within Nigeria and beyond. Our products are reknowned to be of the highest quality that you can find in the market with a very reasonable pricing compared to what is obtainable elsewhere in the market. Our team of professionals are working tirelessly to ensure that quality farm produce are available to our partners and customers all year round without compromising in the quality of our products.

Processing and Packaging

We have a team of professional that are skilled and knowledgeable in the industrial best practices that is used to preserve the quality of the farm produce that was purchased from the farmers and our suppliers. We also engage in packaging of our purchased farm produce for onward sales and deliveries of these products to our partners and customers. When you do business with us, you can be rest assured that the farm produce from LekanMath Nigeria Enterprise has been well processed using the industry best practices and our packaging skillset is world class.
