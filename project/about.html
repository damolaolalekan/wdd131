About Us

A brief outline of our history

LekanMath Nigeria Enterprise is one of the top company in Nigeria that deals with the buying and selling of quality cocoa beans. Our products are sourced from the best farms in the country and they are packaged and processed using the industry best practices. We also buy cocoa beans from farmers at a fair price that guarantees high profit for the farmers without compromising the interest of our teeming customers. We have been in the business of buying and selling cocoa beans from over 15 years and we keep getting better and better as the years pass by.
